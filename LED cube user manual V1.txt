----------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT YOU NEED
----------------------------------------------------------------
  - The LED Cube (Arduino + HM-10 Bluetooth module inside)
  - Power supply for the cube
  - (Optional, for advanced control) A phone, tablet, or computer
    with a Chrome-based browser that supports Web Bluetooth
    (Chrome or Edge on desktop/Android). Note: iPhone's Safari
    does NOT support Web Bluetooth, so app control from an iPhone
    is not currently possible.

----------------------------------------------------------------
2. TURNING THE CUBE ON
----------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Connect the power supply and switch the cube on at the main
     power switch.
  2. The cube will automatically start playing its built-in
     light show — no app or phone needed. This is "Auto Mode".

----------------------------------------------------------------
3. USING THE CUBE WITHOUT THE APP
----------------------------------------------------------------
The cube has two physical controls on the base: a TOUCH SENSOR
and a BRIGHTNESS DIAL (potentiometer).

  BRIGHTNESS DIAL
    - Turn it at any time to raise or lower the cube's
      brightness. Works in every mode.

  TOUCH SENSOR
    - HOLD for 3 seconds: switches between Auto Mode (cube
      automatically cycles through all animations every 10
      seconds) and Manual Mode (cube stays on one animation
      until you change it).
    - QUICK TAP (while in Manual Mode): jumps to the next
      animation in the list.

  That's it — the cube will run its full built-in animation
  library (~37 patterns) forever on just these two controls,
  no app required.

----------------------------------------------------------------
4. USING THE CUBE WITH THE APP
----------------------------------------------------------------
The "app" is a web page (dashboard) that talks to the cube over
Bluetooth. It gives you the same Auto/Manual/Next/Clear controls
as the touch sensor, PLUS the ability to send your own custom
light formulas.

  STEP 1 — Open the dashboard
    Open the dashboard web page in Chrome or Edge (desktop or
    Android). Bluetooth must be turned on on your device.

  STEP 2 — Connect
    Click "Connect HM-10 Bluetooth" at the top of the page and
    select your cube from the list that pops up. The status dot
    turns green once connected.

  STEP 3 — Control the cube
    Mode & Command buttons:
      - Auto Carousel Mode (A) — cube auto-cycles animations
      - Manual Mode (M)        — cube holds on one animation
      - Next Animation (N)     — advance to the next animation
                                  (only works in Manual Mode)
      - Clear Canvas (C)       — blank the cube and cancel
                                  anything in progress

    While a command is being sent, the buttons will be greyed
    out for a moment (status dot turns yellow/"busy") — this is
    normal, just wait for it to finish.

----------------------------------------------------------------
5. WRITING YOUR OWN LIGHT PATTERNS (FORMULAS)
----------------------------------------------------------------
The dashboard has a "Dynamic Compiler Formula Field" where you
can type a math formula and the cube will light up voxels
wherever that formula is true.

  Available building blocks:
    X, Y, Z   — position of a voxel inside the cube (0-7 each)
    F         — animation frame number (changes over time, so
                you can make things move/animate)
    SIN(), COS(), SQRT(), ABS()
    +  -  *  /  %
    ==  !=
    &&  ||

  Example (already filled in by default):
    SIN(X) + COS(Y) > Z

  To send it:
    1. Type your formula into the text box.
    2. Click "Transmit & Parse" — the cube will blink once to
       confirm it received the formula (display then goes dark
       while it waits for you to run it).
    3. Click "Execute / Compile (R)" — the cube will blink again
       and then start displaying your pattern.

  Watch the "Hardware System Terminal Output" panel at the
  bottom of the page — the cube reports back after each step:
    - "Arduino ACK (K)"  = success
    - "Arduino NACK (X)" = failed (usually an invalid formula —
      check your syntax and try again)

----------------------------------------------------------------
6. TROUBLESHOOTING
----------------------------------------------------------------
  For any issue with the cube or the app not responding as
  expected, try these two things first — they resolve the large
  majority of hiccups:

    1. Power-cycle the cube: turn it OFF and back ON at the main
       power switch.
    2. Refresh the app page in your browser (reload/refresh),
       then reconnect via "Connect HM-10 Bluetooth".

  If the problem continues after both of these, note exactly
  what you did and what the cube/app showed, so it can be looked
  into further.
